# Protect private information during practice

Remove real customer names, contact details, credentials and confidential
employer information from practice answers. Generalise a situation without
changing the substance of your own actions. Do not upload identification
documents, private applicant records or photographs containing personal data.

InterviewCoach sends submitted answers and job-advert images through an external
AI service when those features are used. Provider processing policies apply.
Local query embeddings do not send the query to an embedding provider. Personal
packs remain in the current browser; current answers are not saved permanently
by the app. Clearing or leaving a session can discard an unsaved answer.
