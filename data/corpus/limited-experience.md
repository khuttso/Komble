# Answering with limited professional experience

You can use coursework, volunteering, a personal project or a student activity
when you do not have professional work experience. Choose a real example that
demonstrates the skill being asked about. Explain your own responsibility and
actions rather than presenting the whole team's work as yours.

For a behavioral question, organise the example with Situation, Task, Action,
Result. Say clearly when the example comes from coursework. You do not need to
pretend it was paid employment. If the result was incomplete, explain the actual
outcome and what you learned. If you have never encountered the exact situation,
say so and explain a reasonable approach without claiming you already did it.
