# Prepare for a customer-support interview

Practise listening carefully, acknowledging a customer's concern and checking
the facts before promising a solution. Explain what you would investigate and
when you would escalate. Do not promise a refund, delivery time or exception
without authority and evidence.

If you do not know an answer, say that you would check the relevant policy or
ask a qualified colleague and give the customer a realistic update. When
prioritising requests, explain the impact, urgency and applicable service rules.
Use truthful examples of clear communication from work, study or volunteering.
