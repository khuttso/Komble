# Discuss mistakes constructively

Choose a real mistake you can discuss without exposing private information.
Explain what you were responsible for, what went wrong, and what you did after
noticing it. Take responsibility for your own actions. Avoid inventing a happy
ending or turning the answer into criticism of another person.

Describe what you learned and one concrete change in your process. For example,
you might explain a check you now perform earlier, if that is true. If the
outcome remained imperfect, acknowledge that. The useful part of a practice
answer is the evidence of reflection and action, not a claim of being flawless.
