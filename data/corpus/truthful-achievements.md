# Keep achievements truthful

Never invent achievements, employment, responsibilities or numerical results.
Describe only what happened and what you personally contributed. If you do not
know a percentage improvement or exact count, do not make one up. Use an honest
qualitative outcome such as completing a working prototype or receiving specific
feedback, but only if that outcome actually occurred.

When a coach asks for more detail, add a real situation, your actions and the
observed result. A suggestion is not evidence that the event happened. Do not
copy an AI-generated success story into your answer as personal experience.
Interview practice helps communication; it cannot guarantee an offer.
