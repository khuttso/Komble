# Structuring a behavioral answer with STAR

STAR means Situation, Task, Action, Result. Start with the Situation: a brief
description of the context. Explain the Task or goal you were responsible for.
Spend most of the answer on your own Action: what you did, why you chose it,
and how you worked with others. Finish with the Result and what you learned.

Use a specific truthful example. A class project, volunteering activity or
personal project can work. If no numerical result exists, describe an observable
outcome without inventing a metric. Keep context short enough that the listener
can understand your contribution. Practise aloud, then remove details that do not
help answer the question. STAR is an organising aid, not a guarantee of hiring.
