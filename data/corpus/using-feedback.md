# Use coaching feedback critically

Read feedback as practice guidance, not a hiring decision. Check that any
strength or criticism is tied to what you actually wrote. Reject suggestions
that invent experience or ask you to claim an unsupported result. A model can
make mistakes even when its response has the correct format.

Choose one actionable improvement and revise your answer using truthful detail.
For a vague example, add the situation, your specific action and the observed
result. Compare the revision with the question. Grounded guidance includes
citations so you can inspect the preparation passage. If the app refuses because
sources are insufficient, ask a more specific question rather than treating the
refusal as evidence that your answer was wrong.
