# Explain your own part in teamwork

Describe the team's shared goal, then identify your own contribution. Use "I"
for actions you performed and "we" for shared outcomes. Explain how you
communicated, divided responsibilities or handled a disagreement. Do not take
credit for work another person completed.

A useful teamwork example includes a specific decision or action, how others
were involved, and the real result. If the question asks about conflict, focus
on the problem and your response rather than blaming a teammate. State what you
would do differently next time. A course assignment is a valid practice example
when it is clearly described as coursework.
