# Explain how you learn

Choose a skill you actually learned. Explain your starting point, the resource
or feedback you used, and a small task that helped you practise. Describe how
you checked whether you understood it. A working example, a test or feedback
from another person is more concrete than saying only "I am a fast learner."

Mention a difficulty and how you adjusted your approach. Be honest about what
you can now do independently and what still requires help. Do not invent a
certificate, project or level of expertise. Connect the learning example to the
question without claiming that it proves suitability for every role.
