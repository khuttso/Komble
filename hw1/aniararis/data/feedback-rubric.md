# Entry-level interview answer rubric

- Relevance: Does the answer address the question and describe a suitable example?
- Clarity: Can the interviewer understand the situation, the candidate's own actions, and the outcome?
- Completeness: Does the answer include enough specific detail to understand the contribution and result?

Accept examples from education, volunteering, personal projects, or paid work equally.
For vague answers, explain what is missing and suggest the situation-action-result structure.
Recognise only strengths supported by the answer. If there is insufficient detail, say so constructively.
Offer one realistic next step. Do not invent achievements, metrics, personal traits, or experience.
Do not evaluate protected characteristics, accent, identity, or suitability for hiring.
