## How we decide

Small things: whoever is doing the work decides and shares anything the other team members need to know. Big things, including scope, stack, and pivots, require consensus among all members. If we cannot agree, we discuss the tradeoffs and ask the instructor for guidance if needed.

## When we work

We meet to work once or twice a week, depending on our availability and deadlines. We agree on the days, times, and whether to meet online or in person at the start of each week through our team chat. One meeting covers progress, blockers, and next tasks; a second meeting, when needed, is for implementation and testing together.

We agree on shared availability windows each week and let each other know when we cannot attend or will be unavailable.

## Contribution

Work is visible in commits and each member's journal. We divide tasks clearly, review progress weekly, and raise uneven workloads during that week instead of waiting until Week 15. Each member remains responsible for their own individual homework submission. For shared work, at least one other team member reviews changes before they are merged.

## When it breaks

Step 1: name the problem inside the team within 48 hours and agree on a concrete next step. Step 2: if it remains unresolved after one week, bring it to the instructor together.

No-show rule: after two missed agreed meetings without notice, we discuss the cause, confirm availability, and redistribute urgent work if needed. If communication or participation does not improve within a week, we ask the instructor for help. Give advance notice of an absence whenever possible.
